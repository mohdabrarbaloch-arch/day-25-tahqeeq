Read the writing,
not the headline.

Paste a news story. Tahqeeq scores how it is written — sourcing, hedging, hype, urgency — and shows you the exact words behind every point it takes off.

It cannot tell you whether a claim is true. It has no fact database. What it can do is show you whether the writing behaves like reporting or like persuasion.
